# Personal Tutor

Personal Tutor is a game-like learning experience that helps a learner study independently at an appropriate level, repair missing knowledge, and ultimately solve a presented problem correctly.

## Language

**Learner**:
A Class 6 student who uses the app to practise mathematics in the MVP. The intended age or grade range may expand after the MVP.
_Avoid_: User, kid, player

**Chapter**:
A curriculum-defined collection of related mathematics skills presented as a selectable section of the learning experience.
_Avoid_: World, course

**Skill**:
A specific, assessable piece of mathematical knowledge or procedure that can depend on other skills.
_Avoid_: Concept, topic

**Prerequisite Skill**:
A skill needed to understand or perform another skill, even when it was introduced in an earlier school year.
_Avoid_: Basic, old concept

**Problem**:
A mathematics task presented by the app for the learner to solve.
_Avoid_: Question, exercise

**Attempt**:
One learner's work toward solving a problem, including any entered answer and submitted working.
_Avoid_: Response, try

**Evaluation**:
An assessment of an attempt that reports what was understood, the first likely error, and the next teaching action while acknowledging uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Grading, marking

**First Likely Error**:
The earliest step in an attempt that probably stops the reasoning from reaching a correct answer.
_Avoid_: Mistake, failure

**Repair Activity**:
A short, engaging teaching interaction for a missing or misunderstood skill that prepares the learner to try the problem again.
_Avoid_: Lecture, remedial lesson

**Mastery**:
The app's evolving estimate of how reliably a learner can apply a skill, based on multiple attempts and learning signals.
_Avoid_: Level, score, intelligence

**Chapter Play**:
A learner-selected practice experience focused on one chapter.
_Avoid_: Chapter test

**Mixed Practice**:
A learner-selected practice experience containing skills from multiple chapters.
_Avoid_: Subject-wide test, exam

**Daily Quest**:
An approximately ten-minute learning session that combines review, appropriately challenging problems, any needed repair, and a final challenge.
_Avoid_: Lesson, daily test

**Transfer Problem**:
A short problem with different surface details that tests whether a learner can independently apply a skill after a Repair Activity.
_Avoid_: Retry, repeated problem

**Boss Problem**:
An occasional multi-step challenge in which a learner may submit photographed handwritten working to demonstrate independent understanding.
_Avoid_: Exam question, upload task

**Learning Interaction**:
A small in-app action—such as choosing, arranging, dragging, or filling a step—through which a learner develops or demonstrates understanding.
_Avoid_: Input widget, question type

**Misconception Hypothesis**:
A temporary, evidence-backed possibility that a particular misunderstanding explains an error; it is not a permanent label applied to a learner.
_Avoid_: Weakness, deficiency

**Guardian Account**:
An adult-controlled identity authenticated through Google or Apple that authorizes access to a Learner Profile.
_Avoid_: Parent user, student login

**Learner Profile**:
The pseudonymous learning identity that owns a learner's avatar, progress, Attempt history, and Mastery estimates.
_Avoid_: Child account, user profile

**Path**:
The visible progression of Skills and challenges through mathematics; all available content remains playable while the next recommended Skill is highlighted.
_Avoid_: Syllabus, course map

**Curriculum Framework**:
An external organization of learning expectations to which the app's Skills can be aligned, such as a national, regional, or school-system framework.
_Avoid_: Syllabus, board

**Curriculum Pack**:
A versioned, publishable collection of Skills, relationships, Problems, accepted solution evidence, and Repair Activities aligned to one or more Curriculum Frameworks.
_Avoid_: Textbook upload, course data

**Learning Visual**:
A structured visual representation—such as a fraction model, number line, area grid, geometry construction, or chart—that helps a learner understand or act on a Skill.
_Avoid_: Generated artwork, hardcoded diagram

**Prism**:
The learner's tutor-guide who orients, teaches, reacts to Attempts, and guides transitions through the Path.
_Avoid_: Mascot sticker, chatbot, teacher avatar

**Learning Journal**:
A record of meaningful learning evidence and personal milestones drawn from real Attempts, Repair Activities, Transfer Problems, and Chapter progress.
_Avoid_: Achievement collection, badge gallery, trophy shelf

**XP**:
A game reward representing completed learning activity; it is deliberately separate from Mastery and must not be presented as ability.
_Avoid_: Mastery points, intelligence score

**Streak**:
The number of consecutive days on which the learner completes the defined minimum learning activity, initially one Daily Quest.
_Avoid_: Attendance, mastery
